## 👋 About Me

Computational neuroscience researcher working on **neural data analysis, brain connectivity, and explainable machine learning**, with experience across EEG, fMRI, diffusion/perfusion MRI, and macaque electrophysiology. M.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering (AI & Computational Neuroscience), Sahand University of Technology, Tabriz, Iran.

### 🚀 What I'm Doing Now

- 🧠 **Latent mean–covariance modeling of macaque dlPFC activity** — a Transformer-based conditional mean model with low-rank latent covariance estimation to study population dynamics and noise correlations during strategic foraging (supervisor: Dr. Neda Shahidi, University of Göttingen).
- 🎓 **Research Mentor** in Computational Neuroscience & Signal Processing at Interdisciplinary Schools (IDS), Tehran.
- 🏥 **Biomedical Engineering Researcher** in clinical neural data analysis at Sina Medical & Training Hospital, Tabriz University of Medical Sciences.
- 🔬 **Research Assistant** at the Computational NeuroCognitive Laboratory (CNLab), Sahand University of Technology.

### 💼 Research Highlights

- **ADHD detection from EEG** — designed an interdependence-based connectivity feature with a sparse-coding classifier, reaching **99.17% accuracy** from frontal-channel connectivity and beta-band activity (2 publications).
- **Alzheimer's disease progression** — built multilayer functional brain networks across 15 connectivity methods on 201 ADNI participants and used SHAP to identify the most informative network features (with University of Cambridge collaborator; under review at *BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making*).
- **Motor imagery classification** — similarity-based representations and deep-learning pipelines for four-class EEG motor imagery/execution.
- **HCP gradient analysis** — cortical functional connectivity gradients during working-memory tasks (Neuromatch Academy project).
